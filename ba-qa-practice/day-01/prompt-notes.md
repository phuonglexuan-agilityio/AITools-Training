# Prompt notes: what made the answers better

| Task | Basic prompt gave | What I changed | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bug summary | A generic paragraph, no next steps | Named the audience (developer), fixed the format (3 bullets), asked for missing information | Short summary plus a checklist of questions for the reporter |
| Explain a concept | A textbook definition | Said I am new to software teams, asked for one coffee-shop example and a word limit | A short answer with a Given/When/Then example I could reuse |
| Drafting | A long, generic email | Gave the exact two questions, the tone (polite) and a word limit | A short email I could edit in a minute |

## Four changes that helped most

1. **Name the audience** ("for a developer", "I am new to software teams").
2. **Fix the format and the length** (3 bullets, under 120 words).
3. **Give one concrete example or the real material** instead of a general topic.
4. **Ask Claude to list what is missing** instead of letting it guess.

## Where the output surprised me

- **Good:** asking "what information is missing" gave a ready list of questions for the reporter.
- **Bad / risky:** in Q1 Claude offered a cause for the bug that the report does not support.
  It is marked as a guess. Lesson: verify before trusting; do not copy a hypothesis into a
  ticket as a fact.
