## Honest status

- Claude created the brief, the artifact and the research draft. Claude cannot create your
  Project or run Cowork from here.
- In the decision doc, the effort column is a **guess**, and the retention numbers found online are
  **vendor-reported** or unsourced. They are listed with warnings and are not used for the
  recommendation.
- The brief does not reuse the invented answers from the Day 2 role-play.

## Skills practiced today

- **Projects (one per client):** all context for a client in one place.
- **Artifacts for briefs and specs:** requirements as a shareable page.
- **Cowork task loops:** research, document and browser work that runs on its own (to try yourself).

## Done when

The Project exists with instructions, the brief is published as an artifact, and you have a
decision doc you rewrote in your own words.
